Wedbook - Account Deletion

Wedbook is a photographer community and event-booking app. Users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open the Wedbook app.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Follow the instructions to confirm deletion.

If you cannot access the app, email desai.nayan.k09@gmail.com with your registered email address or account identifier. Never send your password.
